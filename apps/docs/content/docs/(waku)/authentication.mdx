Prerendered pages are files your host serves, so to protect content, choose one:

- **Use a dynamic source**: [Notion](/docs/content-sources/notion), [Sanity](/docs/content-sources/sanity), [Obsidian](/docs/content-sources/obsidian) with `dynamicSource()`, or a [custom source](/docs/content-sources/custom) with `files` as a function. Its pages render on demand, so your middleware runs for them.
- **Protect the site at your host**: this is the way for a static source like Fumadocs MDX, whose pages are compiled at build time.

## Protect the Whole Site

### Use a Dynamic Source [step]

This example reads pages from Notion on the server:

```tsx title="press.config.tsx"
createPress(notion.dynamicSource()).plugins(notionPlugin({ notion })); // [!code highlight]
```
