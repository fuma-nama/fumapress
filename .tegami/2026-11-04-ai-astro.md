### AI chat on Waku and Astro

- `aiPlugin()` and `mcpPlugin()` work on Waku and Astro, and `@fumapress/ai` no longer depends on `fumapress`.
- The built-in UI docks the chat. Refactored: your own layout in `ui.renderPage` docks it through its `aiChat` prop, with `useAIChat()` and `AIChatPanel` of `@fumapress/ai/components/chat`.
- The `ai` page action opens the chat, `props.actions.ai` in `ui.renderPage`. For a floating "Ask AI" button, render `AIChatTrigger` of `@fumapress/ai/components/chat` there.
- The chat calls `/api/ai` under the base path of the site.
- AI search indexes the pages of every plugin with page text, like OpenAPI pages.
- Refactored: `AIChatLayout` is removed.
