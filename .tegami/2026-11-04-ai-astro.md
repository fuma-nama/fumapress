### AI chat on Waku and Astro

- `aiPlugin()` and `mcpPlugin()` work on Waku and Astro, and `@fumapress/ai` no longer depends on `fumapress`.
- The docs and notebook layouts dock the chat on every page and show their floating "Ask AI" button.
- Added the `ai` page action to open the chat, `props.actions.ai` in `ui.renderPage`.
- The chat calls `/api/ai` under the base path of the site.
- AI search indexes the pages of every plugin with page text, like OpenAPI pages.
- Refactored: `AIChatLayout` is removed, Fumadocs layouts read the chat of `AIChat` around them.
