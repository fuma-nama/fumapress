## Canonical URLs for blog routes

With `site.baseUrl` set, the blog index, tags and tag pages now get `<link rel="canonical">` and `og:url`, like blog posts do. The URLs follow `paths`, the locale prefix and `site.trailingSlash`. With i18n, each route also links its translations with `hreflang`.

The links are added by the routes, so custom `layouts.index`, `layouts.tags` and `layouts.tag` get them too. If your layouts add a canonical link as a workaround, remove it, or those pages will have two.
