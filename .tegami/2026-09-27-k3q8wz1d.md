## Route functions for every language

`createPageI18n()` and `createLayoutI18n()` in `createPages()` register a route once per language, or once without i18n. They replace the `localeRoutes()` and `withLang()` loop of plugins, `withLang()` left `fumapress/internal`. `staticPaths` takes a function of the language.

## Canonical URLs for every page

Pages of `src/pages` and `createPageI18n()` get `<link rel="canonical">`, `og:url` and `hreflang` links like content pages, the blog routes included. Remove canonical links you added to these pages yourself, or they end up with two.

## Blog tag pages without posts respond 404

On dynamic requests, a tag no post uses rendered an empty page.
