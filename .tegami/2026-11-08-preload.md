### Preload links in `head`

- A `<link rel="preload">` or `<link rel="modulepreload">` in `head` is emitted once, near the top of `<head>`, instead of twice.
- The preload of the site's stylesheet uses `as="style"`, so browsers no longer ignore it with a console warning.
